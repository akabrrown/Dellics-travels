1. Executive Summary


Dellics Travels is a mobile-first travel booking platform that lets travelers search, compare, and book flights, hotels, car rentals, activities, bundled vacation packages, and destination eSIMs from a single app. The product draws on Booking.com — the world's largest travel platform with over 500 million annual bookings — as its primary benchmark for conversion optimization, inventory breadth, urgency merchandising, and loyalty engineering. Secondary insights from Expedia, Trip.com, Skyscanner, Hopper, Airalo, and others add advanced capabilities like dynamic packaging, price prediction, multi-modal transport search, and instant eSIM connectivity.

This document is the single source of truth for building Dellics Travels to a production-ready standard. It defines the competitive research behind the feature set, the visual identity derived from the Dellics Travels logo, the complete feature architecture split into MVP (ready-to-production) and later phases, the membership package structure, the full technology stack, system architecture, data model, Stripe-based payment integration, security posture, the end-to-end operational workflows that tie every feature together, and a complete mobile app UI/UX design (Section 17).





Scope of this release


The MVP defined in Section 6 is scoped to ship as a real, working production app: account creation, flight/hotel/package search and booking, Stripe checkout, trip management, price alerts, eSIM connectivity via Airalo, and a tiered membership system. Advanced supplier integrations (GDS/NDC direct airline contracts, white-label car rental APIs) are flagged as Phase 2/3 and are designed around third-party aggregator APIs (e.g. Duffel, Amadeus, RateHawk) so the MVP can launch without in-house airline contracts.





2. Competitive Research


Booking.com is the world's largest accommodation and travel booking platform, processing over 500 million bookings annually across hotels, homes, flights, car rentals, and attractions. Its scale, conversion-optimized UX, and Genius loyalty engine make it the single most important benchmark for Dellics Travels. Every other competitor in this section is evaluated as a secondary or tertiary reference that adds specific capabilities Booking.com does not own exclusively.


2.1 Booking.com (Primary Benchmark)



Scale &amp; Trust: 500M+ annual bookings, 28M+ total reported listings (hotels, homes, apartments, resorts), and operations in 200+ countries — the benchmark for search performance, inventory breadth, and checkout reliability at massive scale.

Genius Loyalty Program: three-tier program (Genius Level 1–3) unlocking instant discounts, free breakfast, and room upgrades. Rewards are applied at checkout, not earned over time — reducing friction and increasing conversion.

Urgency &amp; Scarcity Merchandising: heavy use of real availability signals (“Only 2 rooms left at this price,” “Booked 12 times in the last 24 hours,” “In high demand”) and countdown timers on time-limited deals.

All-in-One Inventory: hotels, vacation rentals, flights, car rentals, airport taxis, and attractions in a single platform — the “Homes” tab sits alongside hotels, giving apartments and villas first-class status.

Flexible Search Patterns: “I don't know my dates yet” and “I'm traveling for work” toggles built into the search bar from the first screen, reducing drop-off for undecided travelers.

Free Cancellation as Default: properties with free cancellation are heavily badged and filterable; cancellation policy is surfaced before checkout to reduce booking hesitation.

Verified Reviews: only guests who completed a stay can leave a review. Properties display category sub-scores (cleanliness, staff, location, value for money, comfort, facilities) alongside the overall score.

Property-First Mobile UX: optimized for thumb-scrolling through image galleries, map pins with price-per-night overlays, and one-tap saved-list creation.

Deals &amp; Secret Offers: member-only “Secret Deals” and mobile-only discounts that require login, incentivizing account creation before browsing.



2.2 Expedia



One Key rewards program spanning flights, hotels, cars, and activities with points redeemable across the whole trip.

“Bundle &amp; Save” dynamic packaging engine that reprices a trip automatically as flight/hotel/car combinations change.

Member-only prices unlocked simply by being signed in, encouraging account creation before browsing.

Trip boards for collaborative planning — inviting others to view or contribute to an itinerary.



2.3 Trip.com



All-in-one booking: flights, hotels, trains, car rentals, and attraction tickets in a single app, removing the need to switch platforms.

Trip Planner: bookings are auto-organized into a timeline/itinerary view across the whole trip.

Real-time alerts for flight delays, gate changes, and cancellations.

Trip Coins loyalty program with a six-tier membership ladder (Silver → Black Diamond) unlocking lounge access, priority support, and travel credits.

Bundle &amp; Save: dynamically discounted flight + hotel packages shown alongside standalone results.

Multi-currency and multi-language support for a global user base.



2.4 Skyscanner



“Everywhere” search — enter only an origin and browse ranked destinations by price when the traveler has no fixed destination.

Whole Month / flexible-date calendar view to surface the cheapest days to fly.

Price Alerts that track a specific route/date and notify on any fare change, plus an app-exclusive “Drops” feed for 20%+ price drops.

A persistent “Saved” list that groups saved flights/hotels into trip-based collections for comparison.

Metasearch-style filtering by stops, airline, duration, and cabin class.



2.5 Priceline



Express Deals / opaque discounted rates: unbranded hotel and flight deals at a lower price in exchange for details being revealed after booking.

VIP loyalty program with cash-back-style rewards on every booking.

Bundle savings that automatically apply an extra discount when a flight and hotel are booked together in one checkout.

Free cancellation badges surfaced directly in search results to reduce booking hesitation.



2.6 Hopper



AI price prediction (“buy now vs wait”) for flights and hotels, claimed up to 95% accurate, based on historical fare-trend modeling.

Price Freeze: pay a small fee to lock today's fare for up to 14 days while the traveler decides.

Price Watch: passive fare/rate monitoring with push notifications, independent of an active search session.

Fintech-style protections bundled into checkout: Cancel For Any Reason, Disruption Rebooking, and a Price Drop Guarantee (refund the difference if the fare drops after booking).

Mobile-only product design — every flow, including payment, is built for a phone screen first.



2.7 Google Flights



Explore map: a world map of live fares from the traveler's home airport, filterable by interest (beach, outdoors, city) and trip length.

Date Grid: a calendar matrix of departure × return dates, color-coded cheapest (green) to most expensive (red).

Price Graph: a multi-week/month fare-trend chart for a fixed route, with a low/typical/high price indicator versus recent history.

Multi-airport search (up to 7 origin/destination airports at once) and nearby-airport price comparison.



2.8 Airbnb &amp; Vrbo



Vacation rentals / homestays as a first-class inventory type alongside hotels — entire homes, private rooms, unique stays.

Host-provided photo galleries, house rules, and amenity checklists (kitchen, wifi, parking, pool) shown before booking.

Instant Book vs Request-to-Book distinction, with response-time badges for hosts.

Guest-to-host in-app messaging thread scoped to a single reservation.



2.9 Agoda



Aggressive urgency merchandising: “only 1 room left”, live countdown timers on deal prices.

PointsMax loyalty currency redeemable across flights and hotels, stackable with member discounts.

Cash-back-style instant discounts applied directly at checkout rather than earned over time.



2.10 TripAdvisor



Aggregated, cross-platform reviews and a 1–5 bubble rating combining traveler photos, ranked “Travelers' Choice” badges, and category sub-scores.

Destination forums and Q&amp;A threads where travelers answer other travelers' questions before booking.

Price-comparison panel showing the same hotel's rate across multiple OTAs side by side.



2.11 GetYourGuide &amp; Klook



Instant-confirmation activity bookings with a mobile e-ticket/QR voucher — no printing required.

“Skip the line” and “Free cancellation up to 24 hours before” badges shown directly on activity cards.

Curated destination guides that bundle top attractions, tours, and transport passes into a single “things to do” feed.



2.12 Rome2Rio



Multi-modal journey search: a single query returns flight, train, bus, ferry, and drive options side by side with total door-to-door time and cost.

Route visualization showing each leg of a multi-modal journey on one map.



2.13 Turo



Peer-to-peer car sharing marketplace as an alternative to traditional rental-car counters, with host-owned vehicle listings, delivery-to-airport options, and per-trip insurance add-ons.



2.14 Kayak



Fare-trend graphs (“buy now vs wait”) and a dedicated price-tracking hub kept separate from live search results.

Multi-product search: flights, hotels, cars, and packages in one query.



2.15 Airalo (eSIM Marketplace)



The leading standalone eSIM marketplace: instant-download data plans for 200+ countries and regions, activated by QR code with no physical SIM or store visit required.

Plan structure travelers already recognize: Local (single country), Regional (multi-country), and Global plans, each sized by data allowance and validity window rather than voice/SMS bundles.

A simple “How eSIM works” 3-step explainer (buy → scan → connect) shown at every purchase point, since eSIM is still an unfamiliar format for many travelers.

Developer-friendly integration surface: Airalo exposes a REST API plus a Node.js-compatible Partner API SDK, making it the natural connectivity partner for Dellics's NestJS backend (Section 6.23).

6. Feature Architecture


Each module below is tagged Ready-to-Production (ships in MVP), Phase 2, or Phase 3. “Ready-to-Production” means the feature is scoped with a concrete data model, API, and UI flow in this document and can be built directly against the stack in Section 9.


6.1 Search &amp; Discovery



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



Unified search bar for Flights, Hotels, Packages, Cars, and Activities with tabbed results.

“Inspire Me” Everywhere search: enter only origin + budget, browse ranked destinations by price (Skyscanner pattern).

Whole-Month flexible calendar showing the cheapest days to fly/stay within a chosen month.

Smart filters: stops, airline, cabin class, star rating, amenities, cancellation policy, price range.

Map view for hotels and activities with cluster pins and price-per-night overlay.



6.2 Flights



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



One-way, round-trip, and multi-city search via aggregator API (Duffel/Amadeus self-service in Phase 1).

Seat class comparison, baggage allowance display, and fare rules shown before checkout.

Real-time flight status: delay, gate change, and cancellation push notifications.

Fare hold: reserve a fare for 30 minutes during checkout (soft reservation) before payment capture.



6.3 Hotels



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



Hotel search with photos, amenities, guest reviews, and cancellation-policy chip on every card.

Room-type comparison with per-night and total-stay pricing breakdown.

Two-stage inventory: soft hold on selection, hard confirmation on successful payment (prevents double-booking).

“Members-only price” banner for signed-in users (Expedia pattern).



6.4 Packages (Flight + Hotel Bundles)



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



Dynamic packaging engine: selecting a flight and hotel for the same dates auto-applies a bundle discount at checkout.

Pre-built curated packages (e.g. “Cape Coast Weekend”, “Dubai 5-Night Escape”) merchandised on the home screen.

Package builder: add Car Rental or Activities to an existing Flight+Hotel bundle before final checkout.

Single checkout and single confirmation/itinerary for the whole package, even though suppliers differ.



6.5 Car Rentals



[ PHASE 2 ]



Aggregator-sourced car rental search (pickup/drop-off location, dates, vehicle class).

Add-on to an existing flight or package booking from the trip itinerary screen.



6.6 Activities &amp; Tours



[ PHASE 2 ]



Bookable local experiences and attraction tickets, addable to any trip itinerary.

Curated “Things to do” carousel per destination, sourced from a partner activities API.

Instant-confirmation booking with a mobile e-ticket/QR voucher — no printing required (GetYourGuide/Klook pattern).

“Skip the line” and “Free cancellation up to 24h before” badges shown directly on activity cards.



6.7 Trip Planner / Itinerary Hub



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



All confirmed bookings for a trip (flight, hotel, package) auto-organized into a single chronological timeline.

Offline-accessible itinerary (cached) with QR boarding-pass and e-voucher storage.

Trip sharing: invite a companion to view (read-only) a trip itinerary via link.



6.8 Price Alerts &amp; Fare Tracking



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



Track a specific route/date or a whole destination; push notification on any fare change.

“Drops” feed: daily digest of 20%+ price drops from the user's home airport.

Saved list: bookmark flights/hotels into named trip collections for side-by-side comparison.



6.9 Dellics Rewards &amp; Membership Packages



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



Points earned on every completed booking, redeemable as trip credit.

Tiered membership packages (Section 7) unlocking lounge partners, priority support, and bonus points.

Referral program: both parties earn credit when a referred friend completes their first booking.

Automatic refunds routed through Stripe Refunds API according to each supplier's cancellation policy.

6.16 Price Prediction, Price Graph &amp; Price Freeze



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION (HEURISTIC) / PHASE 2 (ML) ]



Explore Map: interactive world map of live fares from the traveler's home airport, filterable by trip length and interest (beach, city, nature) — Google Flights pattern.

Date Grid: calendar matrix of departure × return date combinations, color-coded cheapest to most expensive.

Price Graph: fare-trend chart for a fixed route over the surrounding weeks/months, with a low / typical / high indicator versus recent history.

Book-now-vs-wait guidance: at MVP, a rule-based heuristic compares the current fare to its own 90-day cached price history; a full ML prediction model (Hopper pattern) is a Phase 2 upgrade on the same data.

Price Freeze (Phase 2): pay a small fee to lock a fare for up to 14 days, implemented as a separate Stripe PaymentIntent tied to a FareFreeze record with an expiry.



6.17 Vacation Rentals &amp; Homestays



[ PHASE 2 ]



Homestay/vacation-rental inventory (entire homes, private rooms) alongside hotels in the same Hotels search tab, sourced from a vacation-rental aggregator API.

Host-provided photo gallery, amenity checklist, and house rules shown before booking (Airbnb/Vrbo pattern).

Instant Book vs Request-to-Book distinction, with a host response-time badge.

Guest–host in-app messaging thread scoped to a single reservation.



6.18 Multi-Modal &amp; Ground Transport Search



[ PHASE 2 ]



On applicable routes, search results show train, bus, and ferry options alongside flights with total door-to-door time and price (Rome2Rio pattern).

Single map view visualizing each leg of a multi-modal journey.



6.19 Peer-to-Peer Car Sharing



[ PHASE 3 ]



Host-listed vehicle marketplace as an alternative to counter car rentals (Turo pattern), with airport delivery options and per-trip insurance add-ons.



6.20 Deals, Urgency &amp; Merchandising



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



Live scarcity indicators on result cards — “only 2 rooms left”, “booked 12 times today” — driven directly from real supplier availability counts, never fabricated (Agoda/Booking.com pattern).

Countdown timers on time-boxed promotional fares/rates.

Home-screen deals carousel merchandising curated packages and last-minute price drops.



6.21 Community Reviews &amp; Travel Q&amp;A



[ PHASE 2 ]



Category sub-scores (location, service, value, cleanliness) alongside the overall rating, not just a single star average (TripAdvisor pattern).

“Travelers' Choice” style badges awarded algorithmically to top-rated properties/activities per destination.

Destination Q&amp;A threads where travelers can answer other travelers' questions ahead of booking.

Builds on the core Reviews &amp; Ratings module in Section 6.12.



6.22 Cross-Platform Price Comparison



[ PHASE 3 ]



For hotel results, an optional panel showing the same property's rate across partner OTAs side by side, reinforcing Dellics as the trustworthy starting point for research even before booking (TripAdvisor pattern).



6.23 eSIM &amp; Connectivity (Airalo-powered)



[ READY-TO-PRODUCTION ]



In-app eSIM store: Local (single country), Regional (multi-country), and Global data plans, browsable by destination, data allowance, and validity window (Airalo pattern).

Smart cross-sell: whenever a traveler books a flight or package to an international destination, a matching eSIM plan is surfaced on the confirmation screen and in the Trip Planner.

Instant delivery: on successful Stripe payment, Dellics calls the Airalo Partner API to provision the eSIM and returns a QR code (LPA activation string) directly in-app — no physical SIM, no store visit, no shipping wait.

Manual install fallback: the activation code is also shown as plain text for devices that can't scan a QR code from within the app.

eSIM usage dashboard: data-remaining meter and validity countdown surfaced in the traveler's “My eSIMs” list (Section 6.15 / Profile).

“How eSIM works” 3-step explainer shown at first purchase, since eSIM activation is still an unfamiliar format for many travelers.

9.3 Airalo Integration Approach


Airalo — the eSIM marketplace behind Section 6.23 — offers three integration paths. The comparison below is why Dellics uses the Partner API SDK rather than the raw REST API or the WooCommerce plugin.






Option





Fit for Dellics








1. REST API (build it yourself)



Most flexible, but means hand-rolling auth, retries, and response parsing inside NestJS — duplicate work the SDK already solves.





2. Partner API SDK (Node.js)



Selected. Airalo's official SDK is Node-compatible, drops directly into the existing NestJS backend, and ships with typed request/response handling — fastest path to production for a Node.js team.





3. WooCommerce Plugin



Built for a WordPress + WooCommerce storefront. Not applicable — Dellics has no WordPress layer, so this path is discarded.









Integration note


The eSIM Module (Section 10) wraps the Airalo Partner API SDK behind an internal EsimService in NestJS, so the rest of the backend (bookings, notifications, rewards) never calls Airalo directly — only through this one service boundary. This keeps a future swap to the raw REST API, or a second eSIM supplier, to a single-module change.

10. System Architecture


High-level component view (textual diagram):

React Native App (iOS/Android)  +  Next.js Web App

        ↓ HTTPS / REST+GraphQL-lite (NestJS)

API Gateway (NestJS) — Auth guard (Supabase JWT) → Domain Modules:

   • Search Module → Typesense + cached supplier results (Redis)

   • Booking Module → Duffel (flights) / RateHawk (hotels) → PostgreSQL (order ledger)

   • Payments Module → Stripe (PaymentIntents, Billing, Webhooks)

   • Rewards Module → PostgreSQL (points ledger) + BullMQ (accrual jobs)

   • eSIM Module → Airalo Partner API SDK → QR/LPA code returned to app

   • Notifications Module → FCM (push) + Resend (email)

        ↓

PostgreSQL (Supabase, RLS-enforced)  +  Upstash Redis (cache/queues)  +  Cloudinary (media)


Two-stage reservation pattern for both flights and hotels: SOFT hold on selection (SERIALIZABLE transaction, short TTL) → HARD confirmation only after a Stripe PaymentIntent succeeds, preventing double-booking under concurrent demand.

All supplier calls (Duffel/RateHawk) are wrapped with idempotency keys and retried via BullMQ on transient failure; webhook reconciliation confirms final booking state independent of client connectivity.

The eSIM Module is isolated behind an EsimService interface so Airalo is the only module aware it's calling a third-party SDK — booking, payments, and rewards modules interact with eSIM purchases the same way they interact with any other Booking type.

12. Payment Integration — Stripe


Stripe is the sole payment processor for Dellics Travels, handling both one-off trip bookings and recurring membership subscriptions.


12.1 Checkout Flow



Traveler selects flight/hotel/package → backend creates a SOFT hold and a Stripe PaymentIntent scoped to the itemized total (in the traveler's selected currency).

Mobile app renders Stripe's PaymentSheet (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay) — card data never touches Dellics servers (PCI SAQ-A scope).

On PaymentIntent success (client confirmation + server-side webhook confirmation, both required), the booking transitions from held to confirmed and supplier booking is finalized.

On failure or 30-minute hold expiry, the soft hold is released back to inventory automatically via a scheduled job.



12.2 Membership Subscriptions



Voyager/Elite tiers use Stripe Billing subscriptions tied to the traveler's Stripe Customer object.

Tier changes (upgrade/downgrade/cancel) are driven by Stripe webhooks (customer.subscription.updated/deleted) — never by client-side state — to keep perk eligibility authoritative.



12.3 eSIM Purchases



eSIM plans are one-off purchases: a single Stripe PaymentIntent per ESIMOrder, same PCI-minimized flow as flight/hotel checkout.

On payment_intent.succeeded, the EsimService calls the Airalo Partner API SDK to provision the plan; the returned QR/LPA code and ICCID are written to the ESIMOrder record and pushed to the traveler in-app and by email.

If Airalo provisioning fails after a successful charge, the order is flagged for automatic retry and, if still unresolved after 3 attempts, auto-refunded with a traveler notification — mirroring the package-leg failure handling in Section 16.3.



12.4 Refunds &amp; Cancellations



Refunds are issued via the Stripe Refunds API, amount computed from each supplier's cancellation policy (full, partial, or non-refundable fare).

Elite-tier “free date-change” and Voyager “free cancellation on select fares” perks are enforced server-side against the Membership entity before a refund/change is permitted.

eSIM orders are non-refundable once the Airalo package is provisioned (industry-standard for instant-delivery digital goods), consistent with Airalo's own policy — this is disclosed at checkout, before payment.

13. Security &amp; Compliance



PCI DSS scope minimized to SAQ-A: all card entry happens inside Stripe-hosted PaymentSheet/Elements; Dellics servers never receive raw card data.

Row-Level Security (Supabase) enforced on every traveler-owned table (Booking, Payment, Trip, RewardsLedger, PriceAlert).

JWT re-verification on every API request; short-lived access tokens with refresh rotation.

Zod schema validation on all API boundaries; Helmet + Arcjet on public endpoints (search, auth) to block scraping and credential stuffing.

Passport/ID fields (Section 6.15) encrypted at rest (application-layer AES-256), decrypted only for supplier submission at booking time.

Stripe webhook signatures verified on every event; idempotency keys on all supplier and payment API calls to prevent duplicate charges/bookings.

16. Workflow — End-to-End Operational Flows


These are the step-by-step operational flows that connect every module in Section 6 into a working product.


16.1 Onboarding &amp; Authentication



Traveler opens app → browses as Guest (search only, no member pricing).

Traveler taps Sign Up → chooses email/phone or Google/Apple OAuth (Supabase Auth).

On first sign-in, a User row and a free Dellics Explorer Membership row are created automatically.

Traveler completes profile (name, phone, optional passport/ID for faster checkout).

Session issued as a short-lived JWT + refresh token; RLS policies now scope all reads/writes to this user.



16.2 Flight Search &amp; Booking



Traveler enters origin/destination/dates (or taps Inspire Me for Everywhere search).

Search Module queries Duffel aggregator API; results cached in Redis for repeat queries within the search session.

Traveler filters/sorts, selects a fare → Booking Module creates a SOFT hold (30-min TTL) and a Stripe PaymentIntent.

Traveler completes Stripe PaymentSheet (card/Apple Pay/Google Pay).

On payment_intent.succeeded webhook: Booking Module finalizes the reservation with Duffel, Booking status → confirmed.

Confirmation push + email sent; booking appears in the traveler's Trip Planner timeline.

If payment fails or the 30-min hold expires first, the hold auto-releases and inventory returns to the pool.



16.3 Package (Flight + Hotel Bundle) Booking



Traveler selects a flight, then is prompted ‘Add a hotel and save’ for matching dates/destination.

Packaging engine reprices the combination, applying the bundle discount to a single order total.

Optionally the traveler adds a Car Rental (Phase 2) or Activity (Phase 2) to the same package before checkout.

A single Stripe PaymentIntent covers the full itemized package; on success, the Booking Module confirms each supplier leg (flight via Duffel, hotel via RateHawk) with independent idempotency keys.

If one supplier leg fails after payment succeeds, the reconciliation job automatically refunds the failed leg's portion and notifies the traveler and Support Agent — the succeeded leg(s) remain booked.

A single confirmation and itinerary entry is generated for the whole package.



16.4 Membership Package Upgrade



Traveler opens Dellics Rewards → compares Explorer / Voyager / Elite → selects a paid tier.

Backend creates/updates a Stripe Subscription on the traveler's Stripe Customer object.

On customer.subscription.updated webhook, the Membership row's tier and renews_at are updated.

Perks (bonus points multiplier, free cancellation, lounge access) become active immediately and are checked server-side on every relevant booking/refund action.

On renewal failure (invoice.payment_failed), traveler is notified and given a grace period before automatic downgrade to Explorer.



16.5 Price Alert &amp; Fare Tracking



Traveler sets a Price Alert on a route/date or saves a search to a named trip collection.

A scheduled BullMQ job polls the aggregator API for tracked routes on an interval.

When the fare drops (or crosses the traveler's target price), a push notification and in-app alert are triggered.

Tapping the alert deep-links directly into a pre-filled search/checkout flow for that fare.



16.6 eSIM Purchase &amp; Activation



Traveler opens the eSIM tab (directly, or via a cross-sell prompt on an international flight/package confirmation).

Traveler selects a Local, Regional, or Global plan for their destination → backend creates an ESIMOrder and a Stripe PaymentIntent.

Traveler completes Stripe PaymentSheet checkout.

On payment_intent.succeeded, the EsimService calls the Airalo Partner API SDK to provision the plan; Airalo returns a QR/LPA activation code and ICCID.

The QR code is displayed in-app immediately and emailed as a backup; ESIMOrder status moves to provisioned.

Traveler scans the QR code (or enters the manual activation code) from their device's cellular settings — data activates automatically on arrival at the destination.

ESIMOrder status moves to active on first network registration (via Airalo's status webhook) and the data-remaining meter in the traveler's “My eSIMs” list starts tracking usage.



16.7 Explore, Price Prediction &amp; Price Freeze



Traveler opens Explore Map → enters home airport only → map renders live fares to ranked destinations.

Selecting a destination shows the Date Grid (cheapest date combinations) and Price Graph (fare trend for the route).

The Price Intelligence service compares the current fare to its 90-day FareHistory cache and returns a Buy Now / Wait signal.

If the traveler isn't ready to book, they may (Phase 2) pay a small fee to Price Freeze the fare — a FareFreeze record and its own Stripe PaymentIntent are created with a 14-day expiry.

Booking within the freeze window honors the frozen price; on expiry, the FareFreeze simply lapses with no further charge.



16.8 Cancellation &amp; Refund



Traveler opens a booking in Trip Planner → taps Cancel/Change.

System checks the fare's cancellation policy and the traveler's Membership perks (e.g. Voyager free cancellation on select fares).

If eligible, the refund amount is computed and issued via the Stripe Refunds API; Booking status → cancelled.

If not eligible for self-serve cancellation, the request routes to a Support Agent queue with full booking context attached.

Refund confirmation email and push notification sent on charge.refunded webhook.



16.9 Post-Booking Trip Management



All confirmed bookings for overlapping dates/destination auto-group into a single Trip in the itinerary timeline.

Flight status updates (delay/gate change) push in real time via the airline aggregator's status webhook.

Boarding passes and hotel vouchers are cached on-device for offline access.

Traveler can share a read-only trip link with a companion, or invite them (Phase 3 Trip Boards) to collaborate.

After trip completion, a review prompt (Phase 2) and Rewards points accrual are triggered automatically.
